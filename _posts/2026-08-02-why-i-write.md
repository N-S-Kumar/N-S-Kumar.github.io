Welcome to my website.

I built this space as a home for two connected but distinct writing practices:

1. **Science Notes** — where I document what I am learning in physics, math, and biology, including research ideas and talks I attend.
2. **Reflections** — where I write about literature, philosophy, and personal experiences that shape how I think.

My goal is to think in public, write regularly, and build a long-term record of intellectual growth.
